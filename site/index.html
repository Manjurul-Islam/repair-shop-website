    01Do I pay anything before the repair?

    No. The check is free and you pay only after the repair, when you collect. Courier pickup is , paid on return.

    02What if it costs more than the estimate?

    We send you a quote with the reason and the new price. You approve or decline it on your tracking page or in your account, and we don’t start until you do. Decline, and you pay nothing and take your phone back.

    03Do you need my passcode?

    For most repairs, no. For final testing we may ask you to unlock it in person. We never ask for passwords to your accounts.

    04What’s the difference between compatible and original parts?

    Original parts come from the manufacturer where available. Premium compatible parts are tested high-grade replacements that cost less. Both carry the same warranty.

    05Do I need an account to book?

    No. You can book as a guest. An account shows every repair linked to your number and lets you reschedule or cancel online.

    06Can I change or cancel my booking?

    Yes, for free, any time before your phone reaches us. Use your account, reply to our SMS or tell the assistant.
